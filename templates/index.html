Register / Capture Student Image

Enter the student's name, parent's phone (required), click Capture Image. The server will open the camera window on the host machine. Press SPACE to save.

Recommended format: +{countrycode}{number} (e.g. +919876543210).

  Reload

Images are saved in the server's Training images/ folder as <name>.png. Students' parent contacts are stored in students.csv (columns: name,parent_email,parent_phone,extra).
